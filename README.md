## About Me

I build **reliable software** and the **systems behind it**. I work across the whole stack: **low-level systems**, **backend**, **web and mobile apps**, **AI products**, and **cloud infrastructure**.

I do not just write code. I decide **how a product should be built**: the architecture, the security, the cost, and how it will **scale** when millions of users arrive. I take a hard problem and turn it into a **clean, safe, and simple solution** that a team can run for years.
